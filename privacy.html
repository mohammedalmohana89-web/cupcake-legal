CupCake Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CupCake ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when used on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and store:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Server configuration settings
  • Command usage information required for bot functionality

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide bot functionality
  • Store server-specific settings
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Prevent abuse and misuse of the service

3. Data Sharing

CupCake does not sell or rent user data. Information may only be shared when required by law.

4. Data Retention

Data is stored only for as long as necessary to provide the requested services and features.

5. Data Deletion Requests

Users and server owners may request deletion of applicable data by contacting us through the support server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of any changes.
